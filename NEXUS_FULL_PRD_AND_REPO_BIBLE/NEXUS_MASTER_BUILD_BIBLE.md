
# NEXUS — MASTER BUILD BIBLE

This package contains a full product requirements and engineering specification for NEXUS.

## Product

**NEXUS — The Living Intelligence Layer for the Physical World**

### One-line definition

A live operational intelligence system that turns fragmented real-world signals into a spatial world model, connects impacts, simulates alternative futures, coordinates authorized responses, and verifies outcomes.

---

## Core loop

```text
OBSERVE
→ UNDERSTAND
→ FUSE
→ MODEL
→ CONNECT
→ SIMULATE
→ PLAN
→ AUTHORIZE
→ ACT
→ VERIFY
```

---

## What NEXUS is NOT

It is not:
- a map
- a dashboard
- a chatbot
- a computer-vision demo
- a simple heatmap
- a generic digital twin viewer

The 3D map is the interface.

The product is the intelligence/action loop.

## Strongest demo moment

A citizen uploads a photo of a flooded road.

NEXUS:
1. understands the image
2. geolocates it
3. finds nearby similar evidence
4. fuses the reports
5. updates the 3D world
6. identifies downstream impact
7. predicts what happens if the road stays blocked
8. generates response plans
9. requires authorization
10. simulates/executes the selected response
11. receives new evidence
12. verifies that the situation improved
13. rewinds the whole event for inspection
